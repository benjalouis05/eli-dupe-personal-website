Summer 2026

RESEARCHER — LLM vs. HUMAN SURVEY GAP

GENERAL SOCIAL SURVEY · 2012–2022 WAVES

  • Asked whether the per-question gap between an LLM's implied answer distribution and the real human distribution is a learnable function of the model's own hidden states, and whether that holds for questions the model hasn't seen.
  • Built a frozen evaluation substrate: weighted human and LLM distributions for every ordinal GSS question, broken down by national segment and model size.
  • Measured the payoff as ΔR² over a mechanical baseline under grouped cross-validation, giving a clean go/no-go number.

Summer 2026

RESEARCHER — POLITICAL STEERABILITY IN LLMs

EXTENDING KIM, EVANS & SCHEIN (ICLR 2025)

  • Tested whether the linear "political ideology" direction in an LLM's attention heads can reproduce, and causally control, how real liberals and conservatives answer GSS questions.
  • Replaced named lawmakers with anonymous GSS 2022 respondents, each represented by resampled, verbalized answers from their real survey record.
  • Trained attention-head probes and steered Qwen's generations along the probe direction in a four-probe, three-pole design.

Summer 2026

MECHANISTIC INTERPRETABILITY — LOCALIZING LIES

BUILDING ON "CAN LLMs LIE?"

  • Mapped where deliberate lying, as distinct from hallucination, is computed inside LLMs, using representation-engineering and mech-interp tooling.
  • Also explored brain-encoding models (Meta's TRIBE v2) and LLM agent simulations (AgentTorch).
